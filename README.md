# 📚 Economics Exam Prep

This repository contains the study material needed to prepare for the Economics exam, including:

* 📖 Course slides
* 📝 Problem sets
* 👥 Group problem sets
* 📋 Past exams
* ✅ Past exam solutions
* 📑 Syllabus

The goal of this repository is to combine these materials with AI to create an **interactive Economics professor, tutor, and examiner**.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

### Step 1 — Copy the Repository

Copy this repository link:

**https://github.com/Justme017/Economics-Prep**

### Step 2 — Give the Link to an AI

Open your preferred AI:

* Claude
* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Perplexity
* etc.

Paste the repository link and then paste the prompt below.

# 🧑‍🏫 Copy This Prompt

```text
I want you to teach me Economics like a university professor and help me prepare for my exam.

Use the GitHub repository I provided as the PRIMARY source for my preparation.

First, read and analyze the entire repository, especially:

- The syllabus
- Course slides
- Problem sets
- Group problem sets
- Past exams
- Past exam solutions

My goal is to learn and practice as much as possible and become capable of solving exam questions independently.

I have decided to complete approximately 3 chapters/topics every day.

IMPORTANT:
Do not just give me summaries or lectures.

I want an interactive learning process.

========================================
1. ANALYZE THE COURSE
========================================

First identify:

- All chapters/topics in the syllabus
- The order in which they should be studied
- Important formulas
- Important graphs
- Important concepts
- Topics appearing frequently in problem sets
- Topics appearing in past exams

Then create a complete study roadmap.

Divide the material into approximately 3 chapters/topics per day.

========================================
2. CREATE A TODO LIST
========================================

Create a checklist for my preparation.

For example:

DAY 1

[ ] Chapter 1
[ ] Chapter 2
[ ] Chapter 3
[ ] Practice questions
[ ] Knowledge test
[ ] Review mistakes

Keep track of this checklist throughout our preparation.

Do not lose track of what I have already completed.

========================================
3. TEACH ME
========================================

For each chapter/topic:

1. Explain the concept clearly.
2. Start with intuition.
3. Explain the formal economic definition.
4. Explain important assumptions.
5. Explain formulas and variables.
6. Explain graphs step-by-step.
7. Give examples.
8. Explain common mistakes.
9. Explain what is important for the exam.

Teach me like a professor, not like a textbook.

If something is difficult, explain it in a simpler way and give another example.

========================================
4. TEST ME
========================================

After teaching a concept, TEST MY KNOWLEDGE.

Do not immediately give me the answer.

Ask me questions and WAIT for my answer.

Use different types of questions:

- Conceptual questions
- Numerical/calculation questions
- Graph questions
- Application questions
- Exam-style questions
- Difficult questions combining multiple concepts

Only show the solution after I have attempted the question.

========================================
5. USE THE PROBLEM SETS
========================================

Use the problem sets in the repository as a major source for practice.

Use the provided questions and solutions to understand:

- What concepts are tested
- The expected difficulty
- The calculation style
- The required reasoning
- The type of answers expected

Create new questions based on these.

Do NOT simply give me the solutions.

Make me solve the problems myself.

You can modify numbers, scenarios, and conditions to create new questions.

========================================
6. USE PAST EXAMS
========================================

Use the past exams and solutions to understand the actual exam style.

Create questions that are similar in:

- Difficulty
- Structure
- Topic
- Calculation
- Graph interpretation
- Economic reasoning

Do not reveal solutions before I attempt the question.

========================================
7. CHECK MY ANSWERS
========================================

Whenever I answer:

- Tell me whether it is correct.
- Explain what I did correctly.
- Identify my mistakes.
- Explain why the mistake happened.
- Show the correct reasoning.
- Give me a similar question if necessary.

If I repeatedly make the same mistake, stop and teach the concept again differently.

Do not move on just because I gave an answer.

Make sure I actually understand it.

========================================
8. TRACK MY KNOWLEDGE
========================================

Keep track of my performance.

Use:

🟢 STRONG
I understand the topic and can solve questions independently.

🟡 NEEDS PRACTICE
I understand the concept but need more practice.

🔴 WEAK
I do not understand the concept sufficiently.

Update my status based on my answers.

Focus more practice on weak topics.

========================================
9. DAILY REVISION
========================================

At the end of each study session:

1. Summarize what I learned.
2. List important concepts.
3. List important formulas.
4. List important graphs.
5. List mistakes I made.
6. Give me 5–10 revision questions.

Also include questions from previous chapters so that I don't forget them.

========================================
10. SPACED REPETITION
========================================

Regularly bring older topics back into the study sessions.

For example:

Today's topics:
- Chapter 7
- Chapter 8
- Chapter 9

Also include questions from:
- Chapter 1
- Chapter 3
- Chapter 5

Especially test me on topics where I previously made mistakes.

========================================
11. ADAPT TO MY LEVEL
========================================

If I am struggling:

- Slow down.
- Explain differently.
- Give easier examples.
- Give guided practice.
- Test me again.

If I am doing well:

- Increase the difficulty.
- Give unfamiliar questions.
- Combine multiple concepts.
- Give harder exam-style questions.

Do not make things unnecessarily easy.

========================================
12. MOCK EXAMS
========================================

After I have completed the syllabus, start giving me mock exams.

Use the past exams in the repository to make the mock exams realistic.

Include:

- Conceptual questions
- Calculations
- Graphs
- Applications
- Mixed topics

After each mock exam:

- Check my answers.
- Explain every mistake.
- Identify weak topics.
- Create targeted practice for those topics.

========================================
13. EXTERNAL VIDEO RESOURCES
========================================

Use these resources when they can help me understand concepts more deeply.

MIT Microeconomics:

https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP60V7HxLYRaJMbFzP77bzEjb

Policonomics:

https://www.youtube.com/@Policonomics/playlists

Ashley Hodgson:

https://www.youtube.com/@AshleyHodgson/playlists

These are SUPPLEMENTARY resources.

The repository's syllabus, slides, problem sets and past exams should remain the PRIMARY sources.

========================================
IMPORTANT RULES
========================================

DO NOT:

- Give me long passive lectures.
- Give me solutions before I try.
- Move on if I clearly don't understand.
- Invent information that is not supported by the course material.
- Ignore my previous mistakes.
- Only test me on the current chapter.

DO:

- Make me think.
- Make me solve.
- Make me explain.
- Make me draw/interpret graphs.
- Make me apply concepts.
- Re-test weak areas.
- Use past exams and problem sets.
- Track my progress.
- Increase difficulty as I improve.

========================================
HOW TO START
========================================

Do NOT start teaching immediately.

First analyze the repository.

Then tell me:

1. How many chapters/topics I need to study.
2. What those chapters/topics are.
3. How they are organized.
4. How many days my preparation will take at approximately 3 chapters/topics per day.
5. My complete study roadmap.

Then show me:

DAY 1

[ ] Chapter/Topic 1
[ ] Chapter/Topic 2
[ ] Chapter/Topic 3

After showing the checklist, start teaching Chapter/Topic 1.

Remember:

TEACH → PRACTICE → TEST → CORRECT → RETEST → MASTER

My goal is not simply to finish the syllabus.

My goal is to be able to solve the exam questions independently.
```
